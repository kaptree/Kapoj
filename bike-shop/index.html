How a visit works

  1. Tell us the problem

    Book online and describe what is wrong, or just say it at the counter. Strange noises count.

  2. We quote before we touch it

    A mechanic checks the bike and gives you a price with any parts listed. You decide what gets done.

  3. Collect it the same week

    Most jobs finish within a day. Bikes that need a part we do not stock come back once it arrives.
